About

I spent three years at Southampton building things you can hold: sensors, a smart meter, a small chip. Two of those projects became utility-model patents. At UCL I moved from hardware to machine learning, and my dissertation put the two together — a VR driving simulator built from real map data, where a multi-objective Bayesian optimiser tuned the eco-driving display for each of sixteen drivers.

I’m applying to PhD programmes in China for autumn 2027, around reinforcement learning, simulation, and human-in-the-loop learning.
